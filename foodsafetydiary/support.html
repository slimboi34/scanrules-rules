Common questions

Is my data private?
Yes. It stays on your iPhone and your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
I paid. How do I restore my purchase?
Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
How do I cancel a subscription?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
Is this legal or financial advice?
No. The app gives guidance from public rules to help you understand where you stand. For advice, contact Citizens Advice (free) or a professional.
